Keep your plans safe

Download a complete backup before installing. On iOS, Safari and the home-screen app may use separate storage. If the new app starts empty, use Backup & privacy to import your file. Installation does not synchronize devices.

Wait until Offline copy ready appears before relying on offline access. Save any open edits first. After an update, close all planner tabs and home-screen windows, then reopen. Saved plans and files stay in browser storage; clearing website data can erase them.

MAKE IT YOURS

Your plans, on your terms.

Trips are saved in this browser on this device. There is no account, cloud storage or automatic sync. Other people using this browser can see your plans.

Clearing browser data, private browsing, or switching browsers can remove or hide your trips. Download a backup regularly. Backups, calendar files and prints can contain personal details: keep them somewhere safe.

Browser storage belongs to the website origin, not its URL path. Other sites on the same hostname can access it. The public app does not publish your entered trips or files, but storage is not encrypted.

Save a local copy

Download all trips and their PDF/photo attachments in one JSON file. Nothing is uploaded.

Bring your plans back

Import a planner backup (up to 32 MB including files; planner text up to 2 MB). Review it first; existing trips stay intact. Imported trips are added as separate copies.

No analytics or personal-data uploads. Links open only when you choose them; the linked website has its own privacy policy. Booking and ticket links may grant access to reservations—treat them as sensitive. This app does not make bookings.
